A straightforward sequence to clean up unused accounts first, move smoothly to Metabase Pro Cloud, switch stores to the Free Store Portal, and keep our monthly bill lean automatically.

Week 1: Clean Up & Move to Metabase Pro

Immediate Drop From 413 to 352 Seats

  • 1. Point 5 Automated Tech Alert Emails to a Team Inbox (15 mins): Five inactive Tech staff only get automated email alerts. Sending those emails straight to a shared team address means they need zero paid seats.
  • 2. Turn Off the 61 Inactive Accounts (15 mins): Deactivate non-ExCo accounts that have not logged in for over 30 days (keeping all 14 ExCo members and 5 brand-new hires untouched). Zero charts or dashboards are lost.

Week 2 to 3 & Monthly Routine: Reach 229 Seats

  • 5. Turn Off the 123 Paid Store Logins: Once stores confirm the new link works, deactivate the 123 store seats in Metabase so our monthly bill drops right down to Rp 57.7M/month (229 paid seats).
  • 6. Automatic Monthly Cleanup Before Billing Day: Two days before each monthly bill, an automatic check turns off any non-ExCo account unused for 30+ days. Anyone who ever needs back in is restored in 1 click.
